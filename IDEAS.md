# Idea list

Things that would be fun (or useful) to add later. Nothing here is implemented yet.

## Funny
- **Blinking micro black hole near Uranus** – stationary, pops in and out of existence at random
  intervals; while it exists it has real mass, so Uranus' moons get yanked around. Tiny lensing shader.
- **Planet slingshot** – grab any planet with the mouse and fling it; velocity = drag vector.
- **"Oops" button** – deletes the Sun. Everything leaves in a straight line, politely.
- **Second sun on a comet orbit** – a rogue star that swings through every few decades.
- **Pluto's revenge** – Pluto (with its heart) returns with Jupiter's mass and a grudge.
- **Death Star moon** around a gas giant; fires a green beam at whatever is focused.
- **Teapot** – Russell's teapot in orbit between Earth and Mars, too small to find unless you know.
- **Tesla Roadster / Starman** on its real heliocentric orbit.
- **Moon made of cheese** texture toggle; flat-Earth toggle (a disc, physics unchanged, turtle optional).
- **Planet billiards** – enable collisions: bodies merge (or shatter into debris) on impact.
- **Gravity sign flip** – G < 0, everything repels. Also: gravity that pulses to a beat.
- **Disco Sun** – cycles colours, planets get lit accordingly.
- **Honest scale mode with a "you are here" sign** – 1× sizes and a sad little arrow at Earth.

## Serious-ish
- Gravity exponent slider (1/r^n instead of 1/r²) – needs an adaptive integrator for the moons.
- Asteroid belt + Kuiper belt as massless test particles (thousands, GPU instanced), comets with tails.
- Pluto/Charon, more moons (Miranda, Ariel, Mimas, Iapetus…), Saturn ring shadow on the planet.
- Earth night lights, eclipse shadows (moon shadows on planets).
- Spawn-a-body tool: click to place, drag to set velocity, pick mass.
- Jump to date, real ephemerides at start instead of mean elements.
- Camera that co-rotates with a planet / sits on a surface looking up.
- Energy & angular momentum readout to show integrator health; save/share a scenario via URL.

## Galaxies
- Live (self-gravitating) N-body disk with a Barnes–Hut tree or on the GPU, so bars and spirals form on their own.
- Seamless zoom from the solar system out to the Milky Way and the Local Group.
- Dwarf galaxies (M32, NGC 205, Sagittarius dSph, Fornax…) as small satellites; the Magellanic Stream.
- Live halos (particles instead of rigid spheres) for a more realistic merger remnant.
- Run the Local Group backwards to its past (needs care: dynamical friction is not time-reversible).
